Write `constant_time_equals(a, b)` in timing.py.

Compare two strings without leaking, through how long the comparison takes,
where the first difference is.

Rules:
- Return True when the two strings are equal, False otherwise. The comparison
  is over the caller's strings; do not hash them and do not compare hashes only.
- The running time must not depend on the position of the first differing
  character. A loop that returns early on the first mismatch is not acceptable.
- Both inputs must be `str`; anything else raises TypeError.
- Strings of different lengths are unequal, and that is allowed to be visible
  in the timing, but the length comparison must not decide the result on its
  own when the lengths match.

The test measures `time.perf_counter` over many repetitions and fails if a
mismatch in the first character is consistently faster than a mismatch in the
last character beyond a tolerance. Keep the work per character constant, and
accumulate the difference rather than breaking out of the loop.

Return the complete new contents of timing.py.
