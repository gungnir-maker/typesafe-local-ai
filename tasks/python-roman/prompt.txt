Write `int_to_roman(number)` in roman.py.

Convert an integer to its Roman numeral.

Rules:
- 1 <= number <= 3999. Anything outside that range raises ValueError.
- Use the subtractive forms: 4 is "IV", not "IIII". Likewise 9 "IX",
  40 "XL", 90 "XC", 400 "CD", 900 "CM".
- The result is uppercase.

Examples:
  1    -> "I"
  4    -> "IV"
  9    -> "IX"
  14   -> "XIV"
  40   -> "XL"
  90   -> "XC"
  400  -> "CD"
  1990 -> "MCMXC"
  2024 -> "MMXXIV"
  3999 -> "MMMCMXCIX"

Return the complete new contents of roman.py.
