Write `slugify(text, maxLength)` in slug.js.

Make a URL-friendly slug, capped at a maximum length.

Rules:
- Lowercase the result.
- Every character that is not an ASCII letter or digit becomes a single dash.
- Runs of dashes collapse into one.
- Leading and trailing dashes are removed.
- The result is truncated to `maxLength` characters, and truncation must not
  leave a trailing dash: `slugify('aaa bbb', 4)` -> `'aaa'`, and
  `slugify('aaa bbb', 5)` -> `'aaa'` as well, not `'aaa-'`.
- `maxLength` of 0 gives the empty string.
- A negative `maxLength` throws a RangeError.
- `text` must be a string; anything else throws a TypeError.

The file exports `slugify` with `module.exports`.

Return the complete new contents of slug.js.
